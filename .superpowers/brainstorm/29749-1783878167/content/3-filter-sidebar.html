How should we modernize the Filter Sidebar?

The sidebar is crucial for B2B discovery. How should it feel visually?

A

Seamless & Soft

Remove hard borders. The sidebar blends smoothly into the page layout, separated only by typography hierarchy and very subtle dividers. Custom-styled checkboxes replace the native ones.

B

Contained with Filter Chips

Keep a soft outer border but replace traditional checkboxes with modern, click-friendly "pill" chips for options like Brands or common attributes. Faster to tap and visually distinct.

C

Floating Panel (App-like)

The sidebar sits in a floating container with a soft shadow (matching the new product cards), detached slightly from the main layout. Gives the catalog a very premium, web-app feel.
