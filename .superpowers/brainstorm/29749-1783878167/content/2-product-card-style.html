How should we modernize the Product Card?

The product card is the most repeated element. Which styling direction fits your brand best?

A

Subtle & Minimalist

Flat design with delicate borders (`border-line`). Focus entirely on content. The "Add to Cart" button and quick actions appear gracefully on hover. B2B minimum quantity shown as a subtle gray badge.

B

Elevated & Interactive (Recommended)

Cards have a very soft shadow that "lifts" up on hover (`hover:-translate-y-1 hover:shadow-lg`). Buttons are prominent, and the B2B minimum quantity is highlighted with a clear, color-tinted chip (e.g., Mint or Brand color).

C

Bordered & Structured

Distinct card outlines with internal dividers separating the image, details, and price/action areas. Offers maximum clarity (often preferred by B2B buyers scanning specs) but feels slightly heavier.
